## 4. Moroccan olive-industry context (for editorial copy)

- **Variety**: "la principale variété cultivée au Maroc est la picholine du Maroc qui représente de l'ordre de 90 % des vergers. Elle est à double fin : huile d'olive et olives de table." — OCL journal (EDP Sciences), https://www.ocl-journal.org/fr/articles/ocl/full_html/2014/02/ocl130034/ocl130034.html — i.e. Picholine marocaine, dual-purpose (oil + table), ~90% of Moroccan orchards.
  - Corroboration: "Picholine Marocaine olive tree is the main variety of Morocco, with more than 90% of surface area (700000 ha)." — https://en.excelentesprecios.com/picholine-marocaine-olive-tree
- **Morocco table-olive figures (IOC, 118th session, Nov 2023; 1,000 tonnes)** — https://www.internationaloliveoil.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/118-OT-2023.pdf :
  - 2021/22 final: production **130.0**, exports **76.5**, consumption 38.0
  - 2022/23 provisional: production **128.0**, exports **80.0**
  - 2023/24 estimate: production **120.0**, exports **80.0**
  - **[INFERENCE]** With ~80 kt exported of ~2.65 Mt world production, Morocco is among the world's top table-olive exporters (behind Spain/Egypt/Turkey, comparable with Greece/Argentina) — phrase as "one of the world's leading table-olive exporting countries".
- Editorial hooks usable without company claims: Marrakech/Haouz plain as historic olive basin; Sidi Ghanem as Marrakech's industrial quarter; harvest window Nov–Feb ("The optimal season to harvest olives in Morocco is November to the end of December but could extend to mid February." — https://moroccanoliveoil.com/about/production).
